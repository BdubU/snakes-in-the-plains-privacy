Privacy Policy – Snakes in the Plains

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Snakes in the Plains ("the app"), published by Blaine Wood ("I", "me"), handles information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything it stores stays on your device.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting services, advertising networks, or any other third-party tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, the app saves a small amount of data locally on your device:

  • Your high score, and your best score with each snake skin
  • Which skin you have selected
  • Your sound on/off preference

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to me or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, I will update this page and the effective date above.
